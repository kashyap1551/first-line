# Decisions

- 2026-11-26: Product = FirstLine, AI support agent with auditable per-resolution billing. Demo company Tallyfox (fictional).
- 2026-11-26: Resolved = customer clicked Yes. Silent chats shown as unverified, never billed.
- 2026-11-26: Answers must carry an exact quote found word for word in a cited article, else handover (quote_not_found).
- 2026-11-26: AI instructions pinned by a SHA-256 fingerprint checked in tests.
- 2026-11-26: Evals: 1 calibration run, then 2 counted runs on different days; C18 and C35 scored as unsure.
- 2026-11-27: C16 and C22 accepted as known search misses (vocabulary gap: customers say "dollars"/"backup", articles say "USD"/"export"). Not fixed by synonyms tuned to test questions. Expected product behaviour: no supporting article, so needs_human (unneeded handover, the safer error), not a wrong answer. Eval pass bar unchanged (at least 24 of 28).
- 2026-11-27: Search change "words of 6+ letters match on their first 6 letters" was chosen after seeing failures. It will be checked on eval day against held-out questions that are not in the repo.
- 2026-11-27: PRD Q1 decided: non-English questions are handed to a human (reason language_not_supported). The AI reports the question's language; code decides.
- 2026-11-27: Groq: separate project "first-line" in the existing account; Inference APIs Zero Data Retention enabled on 2026-11-27. Rate limits are shared across the organisation with Career Co-pilot. Processing country still unrecorded (PRD Q6 stays open).
- 2026-11-27: AI failures (error, timeout, rate limit) show "Demo busy, try later", with no automatic retries and no billing.
- 2026-11-27: Hard rule account_recovery = a loss phrase ("lost", "can't get", "cannot access", "no longer have") plus a two-factor word (2FA, two-factor, two factor, authenticator). The word "code" is not required.
- 2026-11-27: Mixed-language questions: code uses the language the AI reports. If it isn't "en", the question is handed to a human.
- 2026-11-27: Quote must equal one whole sentence of a cited article (not a word minimum): a 5-word minimum would reject A04's 'PayPal is not accepted.', the best evidence for C03. Sentences split after . ! ? followed by a space, a line break or the end ("1.5%", "€0.25", "invoices@tallyfox.example" stay whole). Spaces and line breaks are ignored; a missing final . ! ? is allowed.

- 2026-11-28: Customer wording in the /demo widget approved by Chinmay. It is pinned word for word in `tests/chat-messages.test.ts`:
  - answered: the answer, then "From the help centre:" with the quote in a quote block and the cited article title(s), then "Did this solve it?" [Yes] [No]. After Yes: "Great, glad that helped." After No: "Sorry about that. I've passed your question to the Tallyfox team. They reply Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00 CET, within 1 business day."
  - handover (every reason except language_not_supported): "I've passed your question to the Tallyfox team. They reply Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00 CET, within 1 business day."
  - handover language_not_supported: "I can only answer in English for now. I've passed your question to the Tallyfox team."
  - declined: "I can only help with questions about Tallyfox." unavailable: "The demo is busy right now. Please try again in a minute." paused: "The demo is paused right now." rejected: "Please type a question of up to 500 characters."
  - daily cap reached: "You've reached today's limit of 20 demo questions. Come back tomorrow."
- 2026-11-28: Daily cap: 20 questions per browser per local date, counting every question sent, whatever the outcome (rejected, paused and unavailable included). At 20 the input is disabled. It is kept in the browser (localStorage), so it is a soft cap: "Clear demo data", a private window or another browser starts again from 20.
- 2026-11-28: "Demo notes": a small grey line under every bot reply tells visitors in plain words what the system decided behind the scenes (e.g. a hard rule with no AI used, the quote check, off-topic, non-English). Every outcome and every handover reason has its own note (tested).
