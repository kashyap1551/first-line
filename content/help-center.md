# Tallyfox Help Centre (fictional company, invented content for the FirstLine demo)

Format: each article starts with "## A<number> | <title>", then a "Category:" line, then the body. The parser splits on lines that start with "## A".

## A01 | Getting started with Tallyfox
Category: Basics
Create an account with your email address or with Google sign-in. Then open Settings → Business details and add your business name, address and, if you have one, your tax ID. You can upload a logo (PNG or JPG, up to 2 MB), which appears on every invoice. When your details are saved, go to Invoices → New invoice to create your first invoice.

## A02 | Plans and prices
Category: Billing
Tallyfox has three plans. Free: up to 5 invoices per month, 1 user, and a small "Made with Tallyfox" line on PDFs. Solo: €9 per month or €90 per year, with unlimited invoices, no Tallyfox branding, recurring invoices and payment reminders. Team: €29 per month or €290 per year, with everything in Solo plus up to 5 users, roles and expense tracking. Prices exclude VAT.

## A03 | Upgrade or downgrade your plan
Category: Billing
Go to Settings → Billing → Change plan. Upgrades start immediately, and you pay only the difference for the rest of the current billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. If you downgrade to Free, your recurring invoice schedules are paused, and only the first 5 invoices each month can be sent.

## A04 | Payment methods for your Tallyfox subscription
Category: Billing
You can pay for Tallyfox by card (Visa, Mastercard or American Express) or by SEPA direct debit if your bank account is in the EU. PayPal is not accepted. To change your card or bank account, go to Settings → Billing → Payment method.

## A05 | Download receipts for your Tallyfox subscription
Category: Billing
Go to Settings → Billing → Billing history and click "Download PDF" next to any payment. You can change your billing address and VAT ID in the same place. Changes apply to future receipts only. Past receipts can't be edited in the app; contact support if a past receipt needs correcting.

## A06 | Cancel your subscription
Category: Billing
Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. You keep your paid features until the end of the current billing period, and then your account moves to the Free plan. Your invoices, clients and data are kept. Cancelling is not the same as deleting your account (see "Delete your account"). Refund requests are not handled in the app; contact support.

## A07 | Create and send an invoice
Category: Invoices
Go to Invoices → New invoice. Choose a client, add line items with a description, quantity and price, and set a due date. Click "Send by email" to send it from invoices@tallyfox.example with a payment link, or click "Download PDF" to send it yourself. Unsent invoices are saved as drafts.

## A08 | Invoice numbering
Category: Invoices
Tallyfox numbers invoices automatically in order. The default format is INV-0001. To change the prefix (for example to 2027-) or the next number, go to Settings → Invoices → Numbering. A number can't be reused, and an invoice's number can't be changed after it has been sent.

## A09 | Edit, void or delete an invoice
Category: Invoices
You can edit a draft at any time. You can edit a sent invoice until it is marked as paid; your client's link then shows the updated version. To cancel a sent invoice, click "Void". It stays in your records, marked VOID. Only drafts can be deleted.

## A10 | Recurring invoices
Category: Invoices
Available on Solo and Team. Go to Invoices → Recurring → New schedule. Choose the client, the line items and how often to bill: weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly. Each invoice can be sent automatically or saved as a draft for you to check first. You can pause or end a schedule at any time.

## A11 | Payment reminders
Category: Invoices
Available on Solo and Team. Turn on reminders in Settings → Reminders. Tallyfox can email your client 3 days before the due date, on the due date and 7 days after it. You can edit the text of each reminder. An invoice gets at most 3 reminders, and reminders stop as soon as it is marked as paid.

## A12 | Get paid online with Tallyfox Pay
Category: Payments
Tallyfox Pay lets your clients pay an invoice by card from the invoice link. The fee is 1.5% + €0.25 per payment with an EU card. Money is paid out to your bank account 2 business days after the payment. Tallyfox Pay accepts payments in EUR and GBP only. To set it up, go to Settings → Payments; you will need to verify your identity before your first payout.

## A13 | Record a payment manually
Category: Payments
If a client pays by bank transfer or cash, open the invoice and click "Record payment". Enter the amount, the date and the method. Partial payments are allowed: the invoice then shows "Partially paid" and the amount still due.

## A14 | Quotes (estimates)
Category: Invoices
Go to Quotes → New quote. Your client receives a link where they can accept or decline the quote online. When a quote is accepted, click "Convert to invoice" to create the invoice in one step. Quotes are valid for 30 days by default; you can change this on each quote.

## A15 | Add and import clients
Category: Clients
Add a client in Clients → New client. To import many clients at once, go to Clients → Import and upload a CSV file with the columns name, email, address and vat_id. One file can have up to 1,000 rows.

## A16 | Invoice in other currencies
Category: Invoices
You can invoice in more than 30 currencies, including EUR, GBP, USD, CHF and JPY. Set the currency on each client. Reports convert amounts to your home currency at the daily rate on the invoice date. Online payment with Tallyfox Pay works only for invoices in EUR or GBP.

## A17 | Taxes on invoices
Category: Invoices
Add your tax rates in Settings → Taxes. You can use more than one rate on an invoice, and set a rate per line. For B2B sales inside the EU, you can turn on a reverse-charge note that is printed on the invoice. Tallyfox doesn't give tax advice; ask your tax adviser which rate applies to you.

## A18 | Track expenses
Category: Expenses
Available on the Team plan only. Go to Expenses → New expense, choose a category and attach a receipt (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 10 MB). Expenses appear in your reports next to your income. Expense tracking is not available on Free or Solo.

## A19 | Users and roles
Category: Team
Available on the Team plan, with up to 5 users. Invite people in Settings → Team. There are three roles: Owner, Admin and Member. Members can create invoices but can't see Billing. To make someone else the Owner, the current Owner goes to Settings → Team → Transfer ownership.

## A20 | Export your data
Category: Account
Go to Settings → Export. You can download your invoices, clients and payments as CSV files, and download all invoice PDFs as one ZIP file. Export is available on all plans.

## A21 | Sign-in problems and two-factor authentication
Category: Account
If you forgot your password, click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 1 hour. If you sign in with Google, reset your password through Google instead. To turn on two-factor authentication, go to Settings → Security and connect an authenticator app. If you lost access to your authenticator app, contact support.

## A22 | Delete your account
Category: Account
Only the Owner can delete an account. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. The account and all its data are permanently deleted after 14 days. To cancel the deletion during those 14 days, sign in again. We recommend exporting your data first (see "Export your data").

## A23 | Mobile and supported browsers
Category: Basics
Tallyfox works in the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. There are no mobile apps for iPhone or Android yet, but the website works in your phone's browser.

## A24 | Integrations and API
Category: Basics
Tallyfox currently has no direct integrations with accounting software, and there is no public API. To move your data into another tool, export it as CSV (see "Export your data") and import it there.

## A25 | Contact support
Category: Basics
Ask your question in the help chat. If the assistant can't help, it passes your question to the support team. The team works Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 CET, and replies within 1 business day. Refunds, billing disputes, security issues and lost two-factor access are always handled by the team.
